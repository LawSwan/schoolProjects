# SimpleChair - User Stories

## Conference Paper Peer Review Management System
### Multinational Society for Engineering Education (MSEE)

---

## User Stories

### User Story 1: Submit Paper
**As an** Author,
**I want to** submit my paper electronically through the system,
**So that** I don't have to email it to the Program Chair and can receive confirmation that my submission was received before the deadline.

---

### User Story 2: Assign Papers to Subprogram Chairs
**As a** Program Chair,
**I want to** assign submitted papers to Subprogram Chairs,
**So that** the review workload is distributed and each paper has someone responsible for managing its review process.

---

### User Story 3: Assign Reviewers to Papers
**As a** Subprogram Chair,
**I want to** assign reviewers to the papers in my subset,
**So that** each paper is evaluated by knowledgeable experts in the field.

---

### User Story 4: View Assigned Papers
**As a** Reviewer,
**I want to** view the papers that have been assigned to me for review,
**So that** I can access and evaluate them without searching through emails.

---

### User Story 5: Submit Review
**As a** Reviewer,
**I want to** submit my evaluation and comments for a paper,
**So that** the Subprogram Chair can use my feedback to make an accept/reject recommendation.

---

### User Story 6: View Reviews and Make Recommendation
**As a** Subprogram Chair,
**I want to** view all submitted reviews for a paper and submit my accept/reject recommendation with reasoning,
**So that** the Program Chair can make the final decision on paper acceptance.

---

### User Story 7: Make Final Accept/Reject Decision
**As a** Program Chair,
**I want to** view Subprogram Chair recommendations and make final accept/reject decisions for papers,
**So that** I can exercise my ultimate authority to decide which papers are accepted for presentation.

---

### User Story 8: Notify Authors of Decision
**As a** Program Chair,
**I want to** notify authors of the accept/reject decision on their submitted papers,
**So that** authors know whether their paper will be presented at the conference.

---

### User Story 9: Create and Configure Conference
**As a** Program Chair,
**I want to** create a new conference with submission deadlines, format requirements, and page limitations,
**So that** authors know the submission requirements and the system can enforce the deadline.

---

|---|------|------|---------|

| 4 | Reviewer | View assigned papers | Access papers easily |
| 5 | Reviewer | Submit review/evaluation | Provide feedback for decisions |
| 6 | Subprogram Chair | View reviews, make recommendation | Support Program Chair decision |
| 7 | Program Chair | Make final accept/reject decision | Exercise ultimate authority |
